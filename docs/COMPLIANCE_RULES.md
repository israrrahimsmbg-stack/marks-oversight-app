## How RAG works (draft, BR-03 / BR-13)

| Status | Certificate (has an expiry) | Recurring check (done every N days) |
|---|---|---|
| **RED** | Expired | Overdue by more than the grace period |
| **AMBER** | Expires within the amber window, or today | Due within the amber window, due today, or overdue but within grace |
| **GREEN** | Expiry beyond the amber window | Next due beyond the amber window |
| **NOT RECORDED** | No date held. **Never counted as green.** Treated like a red for escalation to the service manager. | Same |
| **N/A** | Doesn't apply to this service (e.g. no hoist, no gas) | Same |

- If the certificate or report shows its own expiry or next-inspection date, **that date wins** over the calculated one (e.g. an EICR that recommends 3 years).
- "Basis" tells the reader how strong the duty is: **legal** (statute or regulations), **guidance** (recognised standard, ACOP, NICE, British Standard), **policy** (Esto Care's own rule). Many items mix them: the *duty* is legal, the *interval* is policy.
- **Duty holder** matters in supported living: the landlord (often a housing association) usually holds gas, electrical and building duties; Esto Care must still know the status and hold a copy. Confirm per service (ELICITATION C10).

## Escalation (draft, BR-08)

| Days overdue (or no date) | Who is told | How [TBC] |
|---|---|---|
| 1+ | Service manager, deputy, team leaders | In-app list / daily digest |
| 7+ (configurable) | Service manager (or their delegate if on leave) and deputy | Email |
| 14+ (configurable) | As above **plus Mark and Hamida** (last resort) | Email, and the item tops the Monday report |
| any | **Support workers: never** | — |

Reminders repeat every 7 days unless the level goes up. Thresholds are the BRD's suggestion; Hamida mentioned "two or three weeks" for head office (ELICITATION D1).

## Known uncertainties (check before sign-off)

1. **Fire drill weekly?** Yasmin said "fire drill once a week". This may mean the weekly alarm test. A weekly full evacuation drill would be unusual. Confirm.
2. **EPC 5 or 10 years.** The Operations app says "EPC 5 or 10 years". Statutory validity is 10 years. Ask where 5 comes from.
3. **Fire alarm 6 months.** Right for BS 5839-1 systems. Some domestic-grade systems (BS 5839-6 Grade D) may be serviced annually. Record the system grade per house.
4. **Electrical (EICR) in supported living.** The 2025 amendment extends the 5-yearly duty to social landlords; existing tenancies were to be inspected **before 1 Nov 2026**. Check each South service's EICR date now: any missing one may already be a legal red for the landlord.
5. **CQC framework change.** CQC is moving from quality statements to 24 key lines of enquiry (pilot Jun–Oct 2026; rollout expected around the turn of the year). Map QA audit headings to the new KLOEs when they are final.
6. **Supported housing licensing.** The Supported Housing (Regulatory Oversight) Act 2023 will bring local licensing and National Supported Housing Standards, with Housing Benefit linked to licensing. Regulations were not yet in force in Nov 2026. Watch for this: it will add licence tracking to the app.
7. **Person-level reviews** (support plans, risk assessments, DoLS, Care Act reviews) are personal data. Hold only a reference, not names, until the DPIA is done.
